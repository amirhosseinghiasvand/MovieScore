How this score is calculated

Normalize: IMDb × 10, Letterboxd × 20, Metacritic stays 0–100.

Confidence: C = N / (N + K), with K = 10,000 IMDb ratings, 2,000 Letterboxd ratings, and 20 Metacritic critic reviews.

Audience: 60% IMDb + 40% Letterboxd, with each source weighted by confidence. Missing audience sources are automatically re-normalized.

Final: base 55% critics / 45% audience, then adjusted once by the confidence of each side.

RT: shown separately because Tomatometer is the percentage of positive reviews, not an average review grade.

Private scraper: Letterboxd is read from its public JSON-LD; Metacritic is read from its public movie page. Results are cached locally for six hours.
